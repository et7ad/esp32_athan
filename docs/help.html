What helps an answer

  • A photo of the clock's screen, or a screenshot of its web page. You can drag pictures straight into the question.
  • Which clock: the colour screen, the one-colour OLED, or an older version 2 clock.
  • Its firmware version: on the clock's web page under System, Firmware Version, or on the menu's Info row.
  • What you tried, and what happened.
