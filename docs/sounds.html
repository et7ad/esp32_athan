Sounds and radio

Every sound the clock offers, ready to listen to here. On the clock, choose one in the menu or on its sounds page (athan.local/audio): it downloads it once and plays it from its own memory.

The library

Number 1 of each list is what a new clock downloads. The tawashih is Random by default: a different one from the list every day, streamed when it plays, so nothing is stored. The clock plays only the left channel; every file here is mono.

A sound of your own

On the clock's sounds page, pick an MP3 file under Upload your own and press Upload. It then shows as Custom in the list and in the clock's menu. A file that is too big, too long or not an MP3 never replaces the sound in use; the page says why.

Make it mono. The clock plays only the left channel, so anything only on the right of a stereo recording is lost. A mono file always plays in full.

The quick way: an online converter

  1. Open online-convert.com's MP3 converter and choose your file (it also takes WAV, M4A or a video).

  2. In the optional settings: Change audio channels to Mono, sample rate to 44100 Hz, and the bitrate to 64 kbps (48 kbps for a tick).

  3. Convert, download the result, and upload it on the clock's sounds page.

Or with ffmpeg, on your computer

Your file stays on your computer. Add -t 300 (5 minutes) before out.mp3 to cut a long recording.

ffmpeg -i in.mp3 -map 0:a -map_metadata -1 -ac 1 -ar 44100 -b:a 64k out.mp3

Radio

Ten slots that follow this project's list, so a station that moves is fixed here once for every clock. Press Up on the clock to play the slot you chose last.

A station of your own

mp3quran.net has over 170 Quran stations by reciter, and the clock plays all of them. To put one on a slot:

  1. Search for the reciter, press ••• on the station, then Copy Link.

  2. On the clock's web page, under Radio Stations, switch Radio 10 follows project list off and paste the link into Radio 10 own link.

  3. Under Radio, choose Radio 10 (own link) and press Play Radio.

The link must be the stream itself (MP3 is safest; Opus, FLAC and WAV work too), not a web page with a player or a playlist file, and at most 255 characters. What makes a link work, and how to play your computer's audio on the clock.
